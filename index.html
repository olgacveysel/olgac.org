nöbet · your servers' night watch

A small terminal app that checks the security of your Linux servers over SSH. It's a lighter alternative to Wazuh or OpenSCAP for teams with fewer than 50 servers.

  • SSH and sudo permissions, open ports, critical updates, user access
  • Scan the local host, or many servers at once over SSH
  • No agents to install on your servers

Systopsy

An autopsy for crashed servers. Systopsy collects logs, kernel messages and service errors from a failed Linux host, rebuilds the timeline and explains why it went down and what to change.

  • AI root-cause analysis with Claude, a local LLM or any AI API

Oneglass · open-source CAASM

Lightweight cyber asset attack surface management (CAASM). Oneglass compares your inventory, monitoring, security and virtualization tools and shows which servers are missing where.

  • Finds VMs that were never registered, monitored or protected
  • Pluggable: turn on only the tools you actually use
  • Terminal UI plus a simple web view

AI, your way

Claude by default. Your model if you prefer.

Systopsy, our next release, turns raw crash evidence into a clear root-cause report. We build and test it with Claude by Anthropic first, and it also runs on a local LLM, any AI API, or no AI at all.

Collect

Gather kernel logs, journald, dmesg, service error logs and system metrics from the crashed host.

Reduce

Local parsers build a timeline and cut gigabytes of logs down to the lines that matter, with secrets redacted.

Analyze

The model you choose correlates events, ranks likely causes and cites the exact log lines.

Act

You get a readable report: root cause, confidence level and concrete config or command fixes.

Choose where the analysis runs

Default

Claude

Our primary, best-supported provider, through the Claude API.

Any AI API

Bring your own key for another provider, including OpenAI-compatible endpoints.

Local LLM

Run a model on your own hardware, for example with Ollama or LM Studio. Nothing leaves your network.

No AI

Built-in rules catch common failures, and you can export a ready-made prompt to paste anywhere.

Why us

Built by someone who gets paged at 3 a.m.

olgac.org is run by Veysel Olgac, a Linux system engineer who looks after production infrastructure every day, with a background in security, monitoring and alerting. He has mostly worked with open-source software, so building in the open comes naturally.

Every tool comes out of that work. Systopsy is for the crash whose answer is spread across five log files. nöbet is for small teams that need a security check but can't run a full security platform. Oneglass is for the VM that never made it into the inventory or the monitoring.
